387 Baird Street

Two stacked three-bedroom flats over an open three-car garage, redrawn from one dimensioned model. Forty-seven sheets, split so each company gets its own: excavation, retaining walls, steel, framing, roofing, plumbing, heating and cooling, electrical and the rest.

Preliminary · not for construction · not engineered

This is a head start for the licensed architect and engineers, who must review it, recalculate and take responsibility for their own sheets. There is no boundary survey, soil report or site visit behind it, and every member size is a stated assumption.

Rev E answers the last round of comments: real measurements throughout, closets built as framed rooms with doors, central air with the ductwork drawn, queen beds and 60-inch tubs, and a single variance.

Three things to know before relying on it

The front building line needs the planner's ruling. The set is drawn at the standard 10 ft. The block-averaging rule may set it closer. The building would then move forward, not grow, because lot coverage caps it at about 975 sq ft.

The drain outlet has about 6 inches of fall. The survey must confirm the French drain reaches the street by gravity. If it cannot, the garage floor is raised; no pump is added.

Do not demolish before the Board decides. Removing the house first gives up its grandfathered setbacks.

What the licensed team needs first

  • Surveyor. Boundary and topographic survey of 387 and 442 Baird together, including the neighbours' front building lines and the street edge.
  • Planning Division, 389 Spruce St, 304-284-7431. Front building line ruling, how the 65 % garage width is measured, and the variance pre-application.
  • Geotechnical engineer. Borings, bearing capacity, lateral pressures, groundwater, rock depth and a mine-map check.
  • Structural engineer. Retaining walls, the steel beam over the garage opening, lateral resistance at the open front, long floor trusses, and shoring beside 441 and 442 Baird.
  • Utilities. Sewer and water main locations and depths from MUB; pole and service type from Mon Power.
