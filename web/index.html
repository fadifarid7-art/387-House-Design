Feasibility concept for removing the existing 1929 house and building a small house over a drive-under garage with storage. Prepared from public records for review by a licensed architect, engineer and surveyor.

Concept · not for construction · no survey or site visit

The lot can take a 36 x 22 ft house over a two-car garage, but only with a rear-setback variance from the Board of Zoning Appeals. Under the R-2 setbacks as written, the buildable area is a wedge of about 396 sq ft. The slope suits the garage: cut in at street level, it puts the main floor level with the back yard.

Ownership and sale history

R & L Rentals LLC has been the owner on every county tax roll from 2005 through 2026. The $160,000 price bought several parcels together in 2000, so it is not the price of this lot. The 2001 entry carries the assessor's code for a transfer between related individuals or corporations. A title search of Deed Book 1246, Page 78 will confirm both.

The county's 2026 appraisal is $28,000: $15,400 for the land and $12,600 for the building.

The lot behind, 386 Forest Ave, is held by R & L Apartments LLC at the same mailing address. Both lots are parts of the original Lot 2, so the seller also controls the rear boundary, which is the tightest dimension on this site.

The parcel

  • 50.0 ft on Baird St, 34.0 ft on the east side, 48.0 ft on the west, with a diagonal rear line of about 46.6 ft (county tax map 09-0029).
  • Three area figures disagree: 2,052 sq ft deeded, about 1,950 from the tax-map dimensions, 1,911 in the county GIS. A boundary survey settles it.
  • Existing house: 1929, two-storey stucco, 24 x 16 ft plus a porch, 768 sq ft, no basement, at or very near the front lot line.
  • The lot rises 8 to 12 ft from street to rear line. Baird St climbs about 11 % along the frontage and is one lane, about 14 ft of pavement in a 30 ft right-of-way.
  • Flood risk is low, with no mapped flood zone.

Get the variance before demolishing

The existing house sits closer to the lot lines than R-2 allows, and that is grandfathered only while it stands. Under section 1373.06, a nonconforming structure that is voluntarily torn down must be replaced to current standards. Make any offer contingent on Board of Zoning Appeals approval.

How many cars

Parking is only possible inside the building. The garage door sits 15 ft from the pavement, but about 7 ft of that is city right-of-way, so nothing can legally park on the apron. The street is one lane with no room to park on it.

Storage for other properties needs a written answer

R-2 allows indoor storage that is accessory to the dwelling. The code defines a private garage as housing the occupants' automobiles, "not commercial vehicles". Stocking materials for other rental properties could be read as a contractor or warehouse use. Ask the Planning Division for its position in writing before buying.

Questions for the licensed team

  • Surveyor. Boundary and topographic survey. The public GIS used here is good to about 3 to 5 ft.
  • Planning Division, 389 Spruce St, 304-284-7431. Pre-application meeting on the rear setback, whether the developed-block averaging rule in 1363.02(C) sets the front setback, whether the garage level is a basement or a storey, and the storage use.
  • Geotechnical and structural engineer. Borings, rock depth, any old mine workings, retaining walls holding 7.5 to 10.5 ft of soil, and shoring beside 441 and 442 Baird and 386 Forest.
  • Civil engineer and City Engineer. A driveway onto an 11 % one-lane street. The street falls about 1.8 ft across the 16 ft door, so the apron has to warp from about 0 % to 12 %.
  • Architect. Fire separation at the east wall, 5 ft from the line, and at the north-east corner, 5.3 ft from the rear line. East, west and north elevations are not drawn.
